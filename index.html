Common Threats Explained

Understanding how modern malware and scams operate is the first step to staying secure.

Trojans

Disguised as legitimate files, games, or utility tools. Once run, they open silent backdoors or download secondary payloads onto your machine.

Ransomware

Malicious software that encrypts your personal files, photos, and backups, holding them hostage until a ransom demand is met.

Infostealers & Spyware

Silently track your keystrokes, siphon saved passwords from browsers, and steal session tokens from platforms like Discord or Steam.

Phishing & Social Engineering

Fake login pages, fraudulent urgent alerts, and deceptive links designed to manipulate users into handing over credentials voluntarily.
